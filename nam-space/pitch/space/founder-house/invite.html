NāM Space · Koh Phangan, Thailand

An Invitation
to the Eight.

Founding member invitation — NāM Founder House

01

There is a kind of person who has done enough of life to know that the next thing isn’t more.

More travel. More success. More cities. More noise.

02

The next thing is somewhere to land.

A place. With your name on it.
Where you can come and go for thirty years
and the door always opens.

That’s what we’re building. And we’re looking for eight people to build it with us.

03

Koh Phangan · Thailand

Forest. Slope.
Ocean in the distance.

What it is

Almost 5,000 square meters of land on Koh Phangan, in the south of Thailand.

Held directly under Thai title because I am Thai. No nominee structures. No grey areas.
On that land, eight units. Each one held by a founding member on a 30-year registered leasehold under Thai law. Yours. Not a timeshare. Not a points program. A real property right, recognized by the Land Department, recorded with your name.
04
The village

We are not building a hotel.
We are not pretending we are.

We are exactly at eight units because that is where the Hotel Act draws its line, and we have no interest in crossing it.

Eight is the number.
Eight people.
That’s the village.

05
Your unit

For thirty years.
Come whenever.
Stay as long as you want.

Your right

Bring whoever you want. Leave whenever you need. The lease is in your name. The Land Department has your record.

The campus

A wellness core. A coworking space that doesn’t feel like one. A café. A studio. Quiet places. Loud places. Trees that were already there before we arrived.

The neighbors

Seven other people who chose this the same way you did. That’s the whole list.

06
When you’re not there

Which, realistically, will be most of the year for most members — we rent it out.

You get 60% of the net rental income. We get 40% to operate the campus, maintain the unit, and run the place at the standard you bought into.

That’s the financial structure. It’s not the reason to do this. If you want yield, there are better instruments.

The rental return is meant to do one thing: make it economically rational to own a place you only use part of the year. It’s a structure that lets the math work without forcing the math to be the point.

07

You’re buying a decision
you no longer have to make.

08
What it actually is

The morning swim. The walk to the café. The desk that’s always yours. The neighbor you didn’t know you needed. The slow Tuesday that reminds you why you started building anything in the first place.

The absence of a search you’ve been running in the background for years.

The coworking, the co-living, the wellness, the workspace — that’s what gets people through the door. It’s the language we share with the world. But what’s actually being built is older and quieter than that.

We’re giving people what they want so we can give them what they actually need.

09
The real thing

NāM is where real magic happens.

The kind that only happens when aligned people come together to build something from the heart. Not because the building is beautiful. Not because the math works.

Because the people in the room actually want the same thing, and they’re willing to do the work to make it real.

That’s the thing. Everything else is in service of it.

Documented. Registered. Paper-clean. 30-year leasehold under Thai law.

But also — and this is the part the brochure usually skips — your presence.

Not all the time. But enough to be real. The eight founding members are the village. You shape what it becomes. You don’t just stay there. You’re written into the walls.

We will ask for your taste. Your honesty. Your willingness to push back when something is drifting.

11
What I am not promising

A guaranteed return

The 60/40 split is real. The rental demand on the island is real. But I am not going to put a yield number on a brochure. That’s not what this is.

A short timeline

Phase 1 designs are with the architect now. Construction will take what construction takes. If you need this to happen on a fixed schedule, this isn’t the right project for you.

A polished experience from day one

NāM Space will be unfinished for the first year. That’s not a flaw. That’s how places find their shape. The founding members are the ones who get to live with that, and to mark it.

12
Honest

I don’t have an answer for everything.

NāM Space is not a fully pre-planned project. It has to start somewhere, and it will evolve from there. I know where we’re going. What I don’t always know is how we get there.

That part we figure out together.

If you need certainty on every detail before you commit, this isn’t the right project for you. If you can hold uncertainty without it turning into anxiety — if you can trust a direction without needing the full map — then come.

13
Who I’m looking for

Eight people who have stopped performing.

Who have done enough to know the doing isn’t the thing.

Who recognize the feeling of arrival without performance and want to come home to it on a regular basis.

Who can read a leasehold contract without their eyes glazing over, but who would never make a decision this size on the contract alone.

Who want a place more than they want a return.

Who are not in a hurry.

14
And for everyone else

I am not on a hero’s journey.
I am on a kinship journey.

If you can’t join the Founder House — maybe the timing isn’t right, maybe the money isn’t there, maybe you’re watching from a distance — I still want to hear from you.

What I am building is too important to come from one person. I need every support I can get. Ideas. Funding. Talent. Vision. If you feel drawn to this, reach out. There is a place for you in this too.

I am happy to lead and to guide. But this should be something we all do together.

15

Write to me.
Tell me why.

The eight will be chosen for fit, not speed. There is no countdown timer on this page because there isn’t one in real life. The right people will find their way here.
